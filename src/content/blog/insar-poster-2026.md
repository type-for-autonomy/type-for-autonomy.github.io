## About INSAR 2026

The International Society for Autism Research (INSAR) Annual Meeting is the premier venue for cutting-edge autism research. Bringing together leading researchers, clinicians, self-advocates, and autism specialists from around the world, INSAR fosters collaboration and innovation in understanding autism across the lifespan. The 2026 meeting emphasizes the importance of co-design principles, accessibility, and practical applications that directly benefit autistic individuals and their communities.

## Our Contribution

**"Insights from the Inside: Ethical Co-Design of Technology with and for Nonspeaking Autistic People"**

Our poster presents critical findings from our ongoing participatory research that centers the voices and expertise of nonspeaking autistic people in developing assistive technology. Rather than designing *for* nonspeaking autistic individuals, we have committed to designing *with* them—a fundamental shift that transforms both our process and our outcomes.

### The Research

Working with a collaborative team of **6 nonspeaking autistic adults who type, 2 support professionals, and 5 engineers and researchers**, we conducted participatory research (summer 2025) following AASPIRE guidelines to develop shared values and refine our project goals. Our analysis identified three critical themes that guide our work:

**Shared Values for Authentic Inclusion:**
- **Accommodations**: Equitable time with flexibility, asynchronous contributions, bite-sized accessible tasks
- **Collaboration**: Presuming competence, listening to everyone, iterating together
- **Respect & Openness**: Mutual trust, honoring diverse lived experiences

### Refined Project Goals

Through democratic collaboration, our project goal evolved to reflect what actually matters to the people we serve:

- **Target User Clarity**: We focus on nonspeakers with pointing experience who need personalized, adaptive typing training
- **Autonomy Over Independence**: Our language shifted from "independent" to "autonomous" to honor individualized definitions of agency
- **Addressing Systemic Issues**: We recognize the complexity of supports needed to reduce both external and internalized ableism

This participatory foundation now guides the co-design of our technology, ensuring that every feature, interface, and interaction reflects the values, expertise, and agency of nonspeaking autistic people.
